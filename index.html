Under the Hood

Engineered for how AI actually reasons

Smart OCR routing for scanned docs
Every document gets read properly, not just the ones with clean, selectable text. Scanned pages and diagram-heavy files are recognized and escalated automatically. Nothing is lost to a bad scan.
Adaptive templates + domain knowledge
Relatch reads what a document is, not just what it says. The structure it builds bends to match — a financial model deserves a different shape than a style guide, because they aren't the same kind of knowledge.
Auto-categorization with relevancy levels
Label uploads as personality, knowledge, instructions, examples, context, or preferences. Each category is reasoned through differently, so your AI weighs what matters the way you would.
Easily injectable in Claude and Codex
Export a .md for Claude or a .zip for Codex CLI. One click, and your AI carries your knowledge into every conversation from here on.
100% quality-gated skill files
Every output is scored before it reaches you. If it doesn't clear the bar, Relatch tries again with more depth — automatically. You get the best result, not just the fastest one.
Private by design
Documents are processed through Relatch's own proxy and discarded the moment the work is done. Nothing is stored, nothing lingers, nothing trains on your content. Every request is authenticated — your data stays yours.
