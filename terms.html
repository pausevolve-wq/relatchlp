5. Document Processing and AI Models

Relatch processes uploaded documents through a multi-stage pipeline. Text extraction is performed using a PDF.js parser for standard documents, or, for scanned and image-based content, an OCR chain of Mistral OCR, then Datalab, then OCR.space, in that fallback order. Enrichment is performed using Google's Gemini API, with OpenRouter (routed to Groq's infrastructure) as an independent fallback provider. A fallback protocol using internal templates is activated if all AI enrichment providers are unavailable.

Relatch includes a smart analyzer that screens uploads before processing begins. Corrupted files, unsupported formats, and documents that do not pass the analyzer's checks are discarded automatically and never transmitted to any external service.

Relatch makes no guarantee that outputs generated by the AI enrichment pipeline are accurate, complete, or suitable for any particular purpose. You are responsible for reviewing all outputs before use.
